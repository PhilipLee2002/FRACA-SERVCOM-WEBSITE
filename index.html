About Fraca Servcom Ltd

Founded on 22 March 2001 in Eldoret, Kenya, Fraca Servcom Ltd began with a modest capital of KSh 12,000. Through dedication and quality service, we've grown into a leading provider of furniture and hardware solutions.

Mission: To provide quality products and exceptional service that meets our customers' needs.

Vision: To be Kenya's preferred supplier of furniture and hardware solutions.

Values: Integrity, Quality, Customer Focus, Innovation.
