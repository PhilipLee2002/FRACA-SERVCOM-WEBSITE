About Fraca Servcom Ltd

Founded on 22 March 2001 in Eldoret, Kenya, Fraca Servcom Ltd began with modest capital of KSh 12,000. We are a manufacturer and seller of high quality wooden and metallic furniture, and high quality ladies and traveling bags in Kenya since 2001 — serving customers from our base in Eldoret and across the country.

From executive office desks and conference tables to beds, dining sets, wardrobes, and institutional seating, we design and supply furniture built for daily use in homes, offices, schools, and places of worship. Our bags range covers travel, school, and everyday carry, all backed by the same focus on durability, fair pricing, and dependable service.

Mission: Quality products and exceptional service that meet our customers' needs.

Vision: To be Kenya's preferred supplier of furniture and bags.

Values: Integrity, Quality, Customer Focus, Innovation.
