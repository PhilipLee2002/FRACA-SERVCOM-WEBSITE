About Fraca Servcom Ltd

Founded on 22 March 2001 in Eldoret, Kenya, Fraca Servcom Ltd began with modest capital of KSh 12,000. Through dedication and quality service, we have grown into a trusted provider of furniture and bags.

Mission: Quality products and exceptional service that meet our customers' needs.

Vision: To be Kenya's preferred supplier of furniture and bags.

Values: Integrity, Quality, Customer Focus, Innovation.
